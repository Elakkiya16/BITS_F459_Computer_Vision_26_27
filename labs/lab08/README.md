## 🎯 Today

Two teams, the same ten tasks, different street photographs. A single-layer perceptron, the
wall it hits, two ways past it, and a race between the teams.

- **Find your team below** and open that team's notebook only. A notebook for the wrong team scores nothing.
- The notebook has **headings and empty cells only**. Each task is read out from the clock on the screen,
  one at a time, and each heading says what to print when it is done.
- After every task, type your result in **your row** of the live board. The projector shows Team A against Team B.
- Personal numbers come from the four digits before the last letter of your BITS ID, so your answers are yours.
